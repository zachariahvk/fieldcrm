Product

Codelash Field CRM, the field sales CRM.

A mobile-first CRM for B2B businesses whose sales happen in person. It covers leads, companies, visits, deals, tasks, quotations, roles and reports: 42 capabilities in all.

Visiting card scanner

A photo of a card or signboard becomes a company, duplicates checked.

Visit logging & tracking

One-sentence logs and geo-verified visits on one timeline.

GST quotations

Your letterhead, the GST split and discount approvals.

AI sales reports Advance

Ask your pipeline in plain language, in Field CRM, Claude or ChatGPT.

Nearby lead generation Advance

Find businesses near your salesperson and add them to the pipeline.

Every capability

Pipeline, tasks, roles, expenses and the rest, all in one list.
