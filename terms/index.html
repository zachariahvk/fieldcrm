2. The service

Codelash Field CRM is an online customer relationship management service for sales teams. It runs in a web browser on phones and computers. What each plan includes is set out on the pricing page and the products & services page.

Field CRM is delivered online. Nothing physical is shipped. After payment, we set up your workspace and send sign-in details to your account admin, usually the same working day.

We improve Field CRM all the time, so features may change. We won't take away a core part of the plan you have paid for during your current term.
