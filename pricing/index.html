    FAQ

    Pricing questions.

    What's included in every plan?

    CLAP, the four things Field CRM is built around: Capture (scan visiting cards and signboards), Log (one-sentence visit logs, geo-verified), Ask (the AI reporting assistant) and Prospect (Search Data, businesses near your salesperson). Every plan also has the pipeline, tasks, roles, CSV import and reminders.

    How do add-ons work?

    GST quotations, the Claude and ChatGPT connector, expenses and attendance cost ₹500 a month each. Add the ones you need to Scout or Explore. Expand includes all four.

    Monthly, quarterly or yearly?

    Pay month to month, or pay for three months at a time and save 25%. For a yearly plan, message us your team size and we'll send a quote.

    Is there a free trial?

    Yes. Start a 5-day free trial at codelash.com/field_crm. You sign in with Google, no card is needed, and nothing is charged when it ends. Choose a plan when you're ready.

    Can we change plans later?

    Yes. Move to a bigger plan, or add an add-on, whenever your team needs it. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll switch it over.

    Do prices include GST?

    No. Prices are shown before GST, which is added to your invoice at the applicable rate, currently 18%.

    Can we get a refund?

    Plan fees are non-refundable once paid, so try Field CRM first: start the 5-day free trial, or book a free demo and see it working with your own examples. If you cancel, the plan stops at the end of the period you've paid for. A payment charged twice or in error is refunded in full. See the refund and cancellation policy.

    Is there a setup or migration cost?

    There's no IT setup: you sign in and start the same day. Existing lists come in through CSV import with a column guide.
