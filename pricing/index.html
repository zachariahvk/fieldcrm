FAQ

Pricing questions.

Is pricing per user?

No. Each plan covers a team: up to 6 users on Standard and up to 12 on Advance, billed annually, with no per-user add-ons and no surprise charges.

What if we have more than 12 users?

Book a call and we'll size a plan around your team.

Can we start on Standard and upgrade later?

Yes. Advance features stay visible to Standard users, with a one-tap request to upgrade when you're ready.

Why is pricing on request?

We share pricing directly for now. Message us on WhatsApp with your team size and where you're based, and we'll send it over.

Is there a setup or migration cost?

There's no IT setup: you sign in and start the same day. Existing lists come in through CSV import with a column guide.
