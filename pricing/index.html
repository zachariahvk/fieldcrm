FAQ

Pricing questions.

Is pricing per user?

No. Each plan covers a team: up to 6 users on Standard and up to 12 on Advance, billed annually, with no per-user add-ons and no surprise charges.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Start a 5-day free trial at codelash.com/field_crm. You sign in with Google, no card is needed, and nothing is charged when it ends. Choose a plan when you're ready.

What if we have more than 12 users?

Book a call and we'll size a plan around your team.

Can we start on Standard and upgrade later?

Yes. Advance features stay visible to Standard users, with a one-tap request to upgrade when you're ready.

Do prices include GST?

No. Prices are shown before GST, which is added to your invoice at the applicable rate, currently 18%. Standard comes to ₹18,000 plus GST a year, and Advance to ₹48,000 plus GST.

How does billing work?

Plans are billed annually in advance, in Indian rupees. The plan renews for another year at the end of each term unless you cancel before the renewal date.

Can we get a refund?

Plan fees are non-refundable once paid, so try Field CRM first: start the 5-day free trial, or book a free demo and see it working with your own examples. If you cancel, the plan stops at the end of the year you've paid for. A payment charged twice or in error is refunded in full. See the refund and cancellation policy.

Is there a setup or migration cost?

There's no IT setup: you sign in and start the same day. Existing lists come in through CSV import with a column guide.
