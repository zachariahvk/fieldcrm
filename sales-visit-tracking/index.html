How it works

Say it, verify it, follow it up.

  1. Step 1

    Say one sentence

    Walking back to the bike or car, the salesperson describes the visit in their own words, in English, Hindi or Hinglish. No form and no dropdowns.

  2. Step 2

    Logged and verified

    AI fills in the type, company, outcome and note, and the visit is geo-verified as on-site or off-site.

  3. Step 3

    The follow-up chases itself

    “Demo next week” becomes a task with email and Slack reminders, and shows up in the morning and evening digests.

Why it matters

After ten visits, nobody fills a form.

Most CRMs ask a salesperson to open the app, pick the activity type, choose an outcome, write notes, set a follow-up date and save, after a full day on the road. So the log gets skipped, or it's written from memory on Saturday, and the CRM describes a much quieter week than the one that happened.

A one-sentence log is short enough to do before the next visit, and geo-verification gives the manager a reason to trust it without a Monday phone call. FAQ

Visit logging questions.

Do salespeople need to speak in a set format?

No. They say what happened the way they would tell a colleague, for example “met Rahul at Acme, discussed pricing, demo next week”, and Field CRM structures it into the log. Typing the same sentence works too.

Which languages can salespeople speak in?

English, Hindi or Hinglish, the way most salespeople in India actually talk. Say “Rahul se mila, pricing discuss ki, demo next week” and Field CRM structures it into a visit log, just as it would in English.

How are visits verified?

When a salesperson logs a visit, Field CRM geo-verifies it as on-site or off-site, so a manager can tell a meeting at the customer's premises from one logged somewhere else.

Which activities can be logged?

Calls, meetings, WhatsApp conversations, emails and notes. You define the outcomes for each, and admins can add custom fields to the log.

Where do follow-ups go?

Follow-ups become tasks with reminders by email and Slack, plus a morning and an evening digest. Tasks filter by today, the next 7 days or overdue.

Is there route or beat planning?

Not yet. Territory and beat planning, with maps and routes, is something we are exploring. So is offline logging for visits with no signal.
