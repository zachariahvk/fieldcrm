What is Codelash Field CRM?

A mobile-first CRM for B2B businesses whose sales happen in person. Reps scan visiting cards into records, log visits in one sentence, and send GST quotations from their phone. Managers and owners get a pipeline, reports and plain-language answers built from what the team actually logged.

Who is it built for?

Small and mid-sized B2B businesses with salespeople on the road, in manufacturing, trading, distribution and services, plus the sales managers and owners who need to see what is happening in the field.

Do reps need to download an app?

No. Field CRM runs in the phone's browser and can be added to the home screen, where it opens like a native app, with no app store and no download. It is built to stay quick on mid-range phones.

Does it work for teams outside India?

Yes. Leads, deals, visit logging, tasks and AI reporting work the same anywhere. A few features are built for Indian businesses, such as the GST split on quotations and reading GSTINs from visiting cards.

Can we import our existing customer list?

Yes. Bring in leads and companies from a spreadsheet with CSV import, which has a column guide, then merge any duplicates in bulk.

How is our data kept safe?

Each account's data is isolated, access-controlled and backed up. Admin, Manager and Executive roles decide who can see and change what, and there is a full activity and audit history.

Does it work without internet?

Not yet. Offline logging, which captures visits with no signal and syncs when back online, is something we are exploring.

How much does it cost?

Two team plans billed annually, Standard for up to 6 users and Advance for up to 12, with no per-user add-ons. Pricing is shared on request: see the plans.
