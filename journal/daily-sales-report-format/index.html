A daily sales report (DSR) is the summary a salesperson sends their manager at the end of each working day: who they met, what happened, what was quoted, ordered or collected, and the next step with each customer. For a field team, the format that works is one row per visit plus a few totals for the day. It fits on a phone screen, and it takes minutes if each row is filled straight after the visit.

This guide gives you that format, a filled example, a version to send on WhatsApp, a free Excel template and the checks a manager should run. At the end: why DSRs get written at 9 pm from memory, and how to stop that.

What is a DSR in sales?

In most Indian sales teams, DSR stands for Daily Sales Report. It records two things: the effort (visits, calls, new customers met) and the result (quotes sent, orders booked, payments collected). For a manager, it's the main view of a field day they didn't see.

In FMCG and consumer-goods distribution, DSR can also mean Distributor Sales Representative, the salesperson who covers a distributor's daily route of retail shops. If someone asks "how many DSRs do you have?", they mean people, not reports.

Two neighbours get mixed up with it. A daily work report lists the tasks any employee did that day. A sales report usually means revenue for a month or a quarter. The DSR sits underneath both: it's the day-by-day record they're built from.

Daily sales report format for field salespeople

A field DSR has three parts: a header, one row for each customer the salesperson met or spoke to, and the day's totals.

1. Header

The date, the salesperson's name, and the area or territory covered that day.

2. One row per visit or call

ColumnWhat to writeExample
TimeWhen the visit or call happened10:15
CustomerCompany name and areaABC Polymers, Naroda GIDC
Person metName and roleMr Shah, Purchase
ActivityVisit, call, WhatsApp or emailVisit
New or existingIs this a new customer?Existing
PurposeWhy you wentPrice discussion
OutcomeWhat happened, in a few wordsWants 5% off on 500 kg
ValueQuote sent, order booked or payment collected, in rupeesNone today
Next step and dateWhat happens next, and by whenSend revised quote by 19 Nov

The last column does most of the work. A row with no next step and no date is a visit that leads nowhere, and a manager can spot it in seconds.

3. Day totals

  • Visits made, plus calls and WhatsApp conversations
  • New customers met
  • Quotes sent: number and value
  • Orders booked: number and value
  • Payments collected
  • Km travelled and expenses, with bills
  • Help needed: a price approval, a stock question, a customer the manager should call
  • Plan for tomorrow

DSR format for WhatsApp

Many small teams in India send the DSR as a WhatsApp message to the manager or a team group. If that's you, keep the same fields with one block per customer, so it reads well on a phone. Writing it in Hinglish is fine; the fields matter more than the language. Copy this:

WhatsApp DSR template
DSR – [date] – [your name] – [area]

1. [Customer], [area] – [person met, role]
[Visit / call] – [purpose]
Outcome: [what happened]
Value: [quote / order / collected ₹]
Next: [next step] by [date]

2. [Customer], [area] – [person met, role]
[Visit / call] – [purpose]
Outcome: [what happened]
Value: [quote / order / collected ₹]
Next: [next step] by [date]

Totals: [ ] visits, [ ] calls, [ ] new customers
Quotes: [ ] worth ₹[ ] | Orders: [ ] worth ₹[ ]
Collected: ₹[ ]
Km: [ ] | Expenses: ₹[ ] (bills attached: yes / no)
Need help with: [approval, stock, a call from you]
Tomorrow: [area, planned visits]

The weakness of a WhatsApp DSR is that nobody can add it up. After a month there are thirty messages per salesperson and no totals. Treat it as a stopgap, not the system.

How to make a DSR report in Excel or Google Sheets

Download .xlsx

If you build your own, these choices decide whether the sheet still gets used in week three:

  1. One long sheet, one row per visit. Don't start a new tab every day. With one list, filters, totals and the weekly report all work. With thirty tabs, nothing adds up.
  2. Dropdowns for activity, purpose and outcome (Data → Data validation). Free text like "mtg", "meeting" and "met" can't be counted.
  3. A real date in every row, including the next-step date, so you can filter what's due tomorrow.
  4. Amounts in their own columns: quote, order and collection, as numbers, not inside the notes.
  5. Totals that calculate themselves with COUNTIFS and SUMIFS by date and salesperson, so nobody retypes them. The template's Day summary and Weekly sheets do this.
  6. Share it instead of emailing it. Upload the file to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets, so the manager sees rows as they're added rather than ten attachments every evening.

Weekly sales report format: the daily report, added up

A weekly sales report is the DSR totalled per salesperson. Keep it to a few columns:

ColumnWhat it tells you
VisitsEffort: is the person on the road?
New customers metIs the pipeline being refilled, or are only old accounts being visited?
Quotes sent (₹)Opportunity created this week
Orders booked (₹)What closed
Collected (₹)Cash in, which in many B2B businesses matters as much as orders
Next steps dueFollow-ups that must happen before next Monday

Read the columns together. Plenty of visits with no quotes, or plenty of quotes with nothing due next week, are both worth a conversation. We've left out benchmark numbers on purpose: a good week depends on your product, order size and territory, so compare each person with their own last four weeks.

What should a manager check in a DSR?

Reading every line of every DSR stops working beyond two or three salespeople. These five checks take a couple of minutes:

  1. Rows with no next step or no date. Each one is a customer who will go cold unless someone acts.
  2. Quotes with no follow-up date. A quote rarely closes itself.
  3. The same customer, visit after visit, with no movement. Three visits and still "interested" usually calls for a different approach, or a call from the manager.
  4. Help requests. A pending discount approval costs more each day it waits.
  5. A day that doesn't add up. A long gap or an odd route is a question to ask, not an accusation.

A DSR works when salespeople see it as a way to get help, not as surveillance. If the only reply a DSR ever gets is a complaint, the reports get shorter and vaguer.

Why DSRs get written at 9 pm, from memory

The format is rarely the problem. Timing is. After six or eight visits, a long drive and a late lunch, the report gets written at home at night from memory, or skipped and written up on Saturday. By then:

  • the person's name and the exact objection are gone;
  • "call next week" has lost its date;
  • the same details get typed again into the Excel sheet, the quote and, if there is one, the CRM;
  • the manager reads it the next morning, too late to help with yesterday's discount request.

A better form doesn't fix this. What fixes it is moving the moment of logging from 9 pm to the minute after the visit, and making the log short enough to do standing next to the bike. There's more on this in why salespeople skip the CRM.

Log the visit as it happens, and the DSR builds itself

That's the idea behind visit logging in Field CRM. After each visit, the salesperson types one sentence, or speaks it into the phone keyboard's mic, in English, Hindi or Hinglish: "Shah ji se mila, 500 kg pe 5% discount maang rahe hain, revised quote kal bhejna hai." Field CRM's AI turns it into a structured log, with the activity type, company, outcome and note, and the follow-up becomes a task with a date.

From there, the DSR's columns fill themselves:

  • Customer, purpose and outcome come from the sentence, and the visit lands on that company's timeline.
  • Where it happened: each visit is geo-verified as on-site or off-site when it's logged, and the route map shows the day's logged stops.
  • Next step and date become a task, with reminders by email and Slack, and morning and evening digests.
  • Quotes are built from the same record as GST quotations on your letterhead, included in Explore and Expand.
  • Expenses: AI reads a photo or PDF of the bill, and the claim goes for approval. Included in Expand.
  • The totals: instead of a summary sheet, the manager asks the AI reporting assistant something like "how many meetings did Priya log this week, and what came of them?", or opens reports by member, source or outcome.
  • No signal: a visit logged without network is saved on the phone and syncs when it's back online.

In short

A daily sales report for field salespeople needs one row per visit, with the customer, person met, purpose, outcome, any quote, order or payment, and a dated next step. Add the day's totals, km, expenses and the help needed. Keep it in one sheet rather than thirty tabs or WhatsApp messages, and fill each row right after the visit. Frequently asked questions

What is the full form of DSR in sales?

DSR usually stands for Daily Sales Report: the summary a salesperson sends their manager at the end of each working day. In FMCG distribution, DSR can also mean Distributor Sales Representative, the salesperson who covers a distributor's route of retail shops. The context tells you which one is meant.

How do I write a daily sales report?

Write one line for each customer you met or spoke to: the customer, the person you met, why you went, what happened, any quote, order or payment, and the next step with a date. Then add the day's totals, your km and expenses, and anything you need from your manager. Fill each line straight after the visit, not at night.

What should a daily sales report include?

For a field salesperson: the date, name and area; one row per visit or call with the customer, person met, purpose, outcome, quote or order value, payment collected, next step and next-step date; and the day's totals, km travelled, expenses and any help needed.

Is a daily sales report the same as a daily work report?

Not quite. A daily work report lists the tasks any employee did that day. A daily sales report is built around customers and money: who was met, what was quoted, ordered or collected, and what happens next with each customer.

Can salespeople send their DSR on WhatsApp?

Many teams in India do, and it works for a small team. The catch is that WhatsApp messages can't be totalled, filtered or searched later. Use the WhatsApp format in this article if you need it today, and move the same fields into a sheet or a CRM as the team grows.

What is the difference between a daily and a weekly sales report?

A daily report records each visit and its next step on the day it happens. A weekly report adds those days up per salesperson, with visits, new customers, quotes, orders and collections, so a manager can see trends rather than single visits.
